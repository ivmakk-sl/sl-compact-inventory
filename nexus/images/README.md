# Images

Nexus page images, tracked in the repo. The background is 1300x372, the gallery images are 1920x1080. The mods-grid thumbnail is not a separate file: Nexus picks it from the uploaded gallery images. The source screenshots are 3840x2160.

- `banner.jpg` - the mod page background image (1300x372): the top rows of the Storage Rack grid, cropped from the "with mod" Day 29 screenshot, every item 1x1.
- `sl_compact_inventory_thumb.jpg` - title card (1920x1080): a "Compact Inventory" wordmark over a crop of the Storage Rack grid from the "with mod" Day 29 screenshot, every item 1x1. Pick this as the mods-grid thumbnail.
- `sl_compact_inventory_before.jpg` - before (1920x1080, source `SurvivalLog_mDRiRVBpc5.jpg`): the backpack and Storage Rack without the mod, same save and day (Day 29) as the "after" shot, items in their normal sizes (circuit boards, a crate, and a dumbbell take multiple cells).
- `sl_compact_inventory_after.jpg` - after (1920x1080, source `SurvivalLog_J4gM3ETBrx.jpg`): the backpack and Storage Rack with the mod, Day 29, every item 1x1.
- `sl_compact_inventory_s2.jpg` - second with-mod shot (1920x1080, source `SurvivalLog_xR6cqNVChp.jpg`): the backpack and Storage Rack with the mod, Day 22, every item 1x1.

Not used: `SurvivalLog_HjZ608kVDv.jpg` (items dropped on the floor after an uninstall test) - kept out of the page, it documents the uninstall risk covered in the README instead.
