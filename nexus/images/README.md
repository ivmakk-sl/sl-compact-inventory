# Images

Nexus page images, tracked in the repo. The background is 1300x372, the gallery images are 1920x1080. The mods-grid thumbnail is not a separate file: Nexus picks it from the uploaded gallery images. The images were prepared by hand from in-game screenshots of the character backpack, Day 29, the same save without and with the mod.

- `banner.jpg` - the mod page background image (1300x372): the backpack without the mod (items in their normal sizes) and an arrow to the backpack with the mod (every item 1x1), faded at the bottom.
- `sl-compact-inventory-thumb.jpg` - title card (1920x1080): the "Compact Inventory" wordmark and "Every Item 1x1" beside the backpack with the mod. Pick this as the mods-grid thumbnail.
- `sl-compact-inventory-comparison.jpg` - before and after (1920x1080): the backpack without the mod (circuit boards, a crate, and a dumbbell take multiple cells) beside the backpack with the mod, every item 1x1.

Not used: `SurvivalLog_HjZ608kVDv.jpg` (items dropped on the floor after an uninstall test) - kept out of the page, it documents the uninstall risk covered in the README instead.
